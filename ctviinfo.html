history

Staten Island Community Television (SICTV) was founded in 1983. Its interactive channel (CTVi) started in 1992 and ran until 2000. Broadcast on channel 56 in Staten Island, New York, the interactive channel allowed viewers to call in live to play games, vote in polls, and receive real-time community information. Calling the local number 727-0235 would give viewers control over the channel, giving them access to nested menus with 3500+ screens. This website is modeled after their 1998 era website! RESPONSE TELEVISON

Channel 56 ran on an Amiga 2000 using a program called Response Television. Made by the Response Television Corporation in Iowa City, a signature of the program was the “Shot Clock” bar at the top of the screen when calling in that would limit your time in the system and the amount of time you had to input a choice shown on the screen. The RT program was a Hypercard-like program that categorized its cards with the routes (for example, a card that was the first choice in 3 subsequent menus would be named “A111”) and used the Amiga Say command for the phone inputs. Other interactive information channels like Iowa City Public Access, Contra Costa County Public Access, and Miami Valley Ohio’s Quest TV all ran on Response Television software.

REVIVAL EFFORTS

Revival efforts have been numerous. I have contacted the creator of Response Television, Staten Island Community Media, various television channels that also used Response Television, have run scans of the Wayback Machine and tried to dig through the 2009 Geocities Torrent to try and find the few remains of this channel. We're making headway but any help or leads are welcome! If you have ANY information, please reach out via email.

CHOOSE YOUR OWN ADVENTURES

By far the most beloved part of CTV-i, three choose your own adventures (The Adventure of You, Your Haunted Adventure, and a Space adventure with a name lost to time) were a popular pastime for Staten Islanders in the 90s. We currently have a WIP script for two of the three adventures, check it out here and let us know if you can fill in any blanks!
